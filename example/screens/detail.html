Miso-glazed salmon

⏱ 30 min🍽 2 servings📶 easy

A weeknight staple: sweet-savory miso glaze, crisp skin, and rice you can start while the fish marinates. Everything happens in one pan.

Ingredients

Salmon fillets2 × 150 g
White miso paste2 tbsp
Mirin1 tbsp
Soy sauce1 tbsp
Brown sugar1 tsp
Grated ginger1 tsp
Neutral oil1 tbsp
Spring onions2
Sesame seeds1 tsp
Short-grain rice180 g
Lime½
Steamed greensto serve

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then cook it — it will be ready just as the fish comes out of the pan.
  2. Whisk the miso, mirin, soy sauce, sugar, and ginger into a smooth glaze in a small bowl.
  3. Pat the salmon dry, season lightly, and brush half the glaze over the flesh side of each fillet.
  4. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat. Lay the fillets in skin-side down and cook for 4 minutes without moving them, so the skin crisps.
  5. Flip carefully, spoon over the remaining glaze, and cook 2–3 more minutes until the glaze bubbles and the fish flakes at the edges.
  6. Rest for 2 minutes. Serve over rice with spring onions, sesame seeds, a squeeze of lime, and the greens on the side.

Nutrition per serving

520kcal
38 gprotein
44 gcarbs
19 gfat

Chef's note

If your glaze darkens too fast, drop the heat — miso burns quicker than you think. Leftover glaze keeps in the fridge for a week and works on eggplant, chicken, or tofu.
